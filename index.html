Biography

I’m a Sr. Computer Vision engineer at Hover where I work on 3D reconstruction, structure-from-motion and general 3D vision. I was previously at Niantic also working on visual relocalization and 3D reconstruction. I hold a PhD from the University of Warwick (UK) in the Intelligent Vehicles group. My research focused on 3D vision for autonomous driving, including cooperative 3D object detection, point cloud registration and sensor pose optimisation.
